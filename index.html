Start a Round

A reaction game. Moles appear at random across a three-by-three board, and each one you hit before it disappears adds to your score.

How to Play

  1. Choose a difficulty and decide whether sound is on.
  2. Select Start Game to begin a timed round.
  3. Hit each mole that appears before it disappears again.
  4. Use the mouse, a touch screen, or the keyboard. Tab moves between holes, and Enter or Space hits the selected hole.
  5. The round ends when the time remaining reaches zero.
